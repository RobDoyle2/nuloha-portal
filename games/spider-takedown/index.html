Team up with a friend on their own phone or computer! One of you taps New room and shares the code.

🎯 How to win

  1. 🕸️Shoot webs at the glowing spots to claim the garden.
  2. 🐜Beat the giant Fire Ant.
  3. 🏠Take over the house and free the caged mouse 🔓.
  4. 🏚️Clear the attic: clean it up, beat the 🦇 bat, then face the Brick Buddies!

💡 Handy tips

  • ⬆️Every spot you claim upgrades your spider.
  • 🌻Walk into a sunflower stem to climb it. Jump twice to peek over the grass.
  • 💎Collect items to unlock hats & weapons.
  • 🐭Find the little mouse in the garden: he joins your team and you can ride him!
